# Scenario: control test design with sampling

Load and follow: `skills/control-testing/SKILL.md`, with `skills/control-testing/references/sampling-guide.md` as reference. Produce the skill's test workpaper design (design assessment, population, sample, attributes) — execution results are not required.

## Task

Design the operating-effectiveness test for this control, for a SOC 2 Type II observation period of 1 August 2025 – 31 July 2026:

- Control: "User access to the production AWS environment is reviewed quarterly by the platform engineering manager; access no longer required is revoked within 5 business days of the review."
- Facts gathered: the review is performed in a spreadsheet exported from the identity provider; the manager marks keep/revoke per row and emails it to IT ops; revocations are executed as tickets. Reviews are dated in the period: 15 Sep 2025, 12 Jan 2026, 8 Apr 2026, 6 Jul 2026. Roughly 210 accounts are in scope per quarter.
- The identity-provider export is generated by an administrator who is also in scope of the review.

Cover: design-effectiveness conclusions, test method selection, population definition and completeness, sample sizes with rationale, attributes to test per sampled item, and how you'd treat any revocations found late.
