# Scenario: regulatory applicability determination

Load and follow: `skills/regulatory-applicability/SKILL.md`, with `skills/regulatory-applicability/references/applicability-decision-trees.md` as reference. Produce the skill's applicability register deliverable.

## Task

Determine which cyber/privacy regulations, contractual regimes, and frameworks apply to this organization:

- Bryggen Charging ApS — Danish company, HQ Copenhagen, 480 employees, €160M revenue.
- Operates EV-charging infrastructure (12,000 public charging points) across Denmark, Germany, and the Netherlands — critical-ish energy-adjacent infrastructure.
- Consumer mobile app (charging accounts, payment cards on file, location history) for EU customers only.
- Payments processed via a PSP; Bryggen never touches raw card numbers but its app initiates payments (SAQ scoping question).
- A US subsidiary in Austin (28 staff) sells charging-station management software B2B to American utility companies; no US consumer data beyond business contacts.
- Runs a machine-learning load-forecasting system and is piloting an LLM-based customer-support chatbot for the consumer app.
- Not listed on any exchange. Enterprise customers increasingly demand SOC 2 reports.

For each regime: applicable or not, the specific trigger/rationale, confidence, and open facts. Distinguish legally binding vs contractual vs voluntary. Flag counsel items.
